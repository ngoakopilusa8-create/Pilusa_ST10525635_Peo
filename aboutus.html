Meet the team

We Provide Only the Best Service

WeCare Medical Centre is here to provide top-quality healthcare in a comfortable and modern setting. Visit us and experience expert medical care from our dedicated professionals.

Medical director

Dr C Saidiya

Dr Christian Nasibu Sisa Saidiya is a Family Physician, medical educator, and healthcare leader with over 25 years of experience in clinical medicine, emergency care, medical education, and health services management. He serves as a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Practice of Medicine at Sefako Makgatho Health Sciences University and is the Founder and Medical Director of WeCare Medical Centre in Pretoria, a multidisciplinary healthcare facility providing comprehensive primary and specialist care. He also provides telemedicine consultations. Passionate about excellence in patient care, medical training, and healthcare innovation, Dr Saidiya is committed to improving health outcomes through evidence-based medicine, leadership, and mentorship.

Our specialist in diagnostic imaging

Dr E Mpofu

Dr E Mpofu is part of the dedicated WeCare Medical Centre team, helping patients access thoughtful, professional healthcare in a welcoming environment.Major (Dr) Erina Mpofu. MBChB(medunsa)FC Rad Diag(SA) Radiologist in the South African National Defense Force (SANDF) and WeCare Medical Center. Completed undergraduate medical degree, MBChB, at MEDUNSA University in 2000 . After internship in 2001 and community service in 2002, I worked as a medical officer in the public sector from 2003 to 2004. In 2004, I joined the SANDF as a Medical Officer and has been a member of the Military Health Service since then, currently holding the rank of Major. I was deployed to the Democratic Republic of Congo in 2004 on a peacekeeping mission and within the borders of South Africa. I completed Aviation Medicine training in 2005 and Military Formative Training in 2009. Driven by a passion for diagnostic medicine, I specialized in radiology at Steve Biko hospital under the University of Pretoria from 2009 and qualified as a radiologist in 2016.

Our specialist in obstetrics and gynaecology

Dr BJ Assumani

Dr BJ Assumani is a highly skilled and compassionate obstetrician and gynaecologist dedicated to providing exceptional care to women throughout their reproductive journey. With extensive experience in managing pregnancies, performing deliveries, and addressing various gynaecological concerns, Dr Assumani is committed to promoting women's health and well-being. She combines her medical expertise with a patient-centered approach, ensuring that each individual receives personalized care and support. Dr Assumani's commitment to excellence and her passion for women's health make her a trusted healthcare provider in the field of obstetrics and gynaecology.

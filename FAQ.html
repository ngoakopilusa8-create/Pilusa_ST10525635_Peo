Frequently Asked Questions

Below are some of the questions patients ask most often about our services and care.

What services does WeCare Medical Centre offer?

WeCare Medical Centre offers family medicine, radiology, physiotherapy, fertility services, IV therapy, laboratory testing, speech therapy, audiology, dentistry, and gynaecology services.

Do I need to make an appointment?

Walk-ins may be accepted depending on availability, but booking in advance is strongly recommended to reduce waiting time.

Do you accept medical aid?

Yes, we accept most major medical aid providers. Please bring your medical aid card and ID when visiting.

What if I do not have medical aid?

We offer affordable consultation rates for self-paying patients. Card or cash payments are accepted.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Please bring your ID, medical aid card if applicable, and a list of any current medication or previous medical records relevant to your visit.

Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?

Yes. Please contact the clinic at least 24 hours in advance to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations may attract a charge.

Are my medical records confidential?

Yes. Your personal and medical information is protected under confidentiality policies and POPIA guidelines.

Do you provide after-hours or emergency care?

Emergency consultation support may be arranged depending on the situation. For urgent gynaecological support outside normal business hours, contact the designated number provided by the clinic.

How do I get my test results?

Test results are usually available within a few days depending on the type of test. Our team will contact you when they are ready or advise if a follow-up consultation is needed.

Can I choose a specific doctor or specialist?

Yes. You may request a specific doctor or clinician based on your needs, subject to availability.

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does WeCare Medical Centre offer? ▼

WeCare Medical Centre offers a range of healthcare services including Dentistry, Family Medicine, Gynaecology, IV Therapy, Men's Health, Pathology, Physiotherapy, Radiology and Speech Therapy & Audiology. The specific services available to you will be determined based on your medical needs and the availability of our healthcare professionals.

Do I need to make an appointment? ▼

While walk-ins are welcome, making an appointment in advance is strongly recommended to minimize wait times.

Do you accept medical aid? ▼

Yes, we accept most major medical aids. Please present your medical aid card during check-in.

What if I don't have medical aid? ▼

We offer affordable private consultation rates for self-paying patients. Payment can be made via card or cash.

What documents should I bring to my appointment? ▼

Please bring a valid ID, your medical aid card (if applicable), and any relevant medical history or medication lists.

Are follow-up consultations included in the initial consultation fee? ▼

No, follow-up consultations are generally billed separately unless otherwise specified by your practitioner.

How do I cancel or reschedule an appointment? ▼

Please contact us at least 24 hours in advance to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations or missed appointments may incur a cancellation fee.

Are my medical records confidential? ▼

Yes. Your personal and medical information is protected in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). We will not share your data without your consent unless required by law.

Do you offer after-hours/emergency services? ▼

After-hours and emergency services are only offered by our gynaecologist. If you require urgent gynaecological assistance outside of regular hours, please contact: 076 357 8301.

How can I request a repeat prescription? ▼

Repeat prescriptions may be requested for chronic conditions already managed by our doctors. You may need a follow-up consultation depending on the condition and time since your last visit.

How do I get my test results? ▼

Test results are usually available within a few days, depending on the test. You will be contacted to schedule a follow-up or you may call the practice to check if your results are ready. Some results require an in-person consultation.

Can I choose a specific doctor? ▼

We currently have one practitioner per discipline. You choose the practitioner based on your medical needs.

How do I update my personal or medical aid details? ▼

Please inform our reception staff as soon as any of your personal, contact, or medical aid information changes to ensure your account and medical records remain accurate.

What happens if I'm late for my appointment? ▼

If you arrive late, we will do our best to accommodate you. However, patients who arrive more than 15 minutes late may need to reschedule depending on the doctor's schedule.

Can I request my medical records? ▼

Yes. You may request a copy of your records by submitting a written request. There may be a fee, and processing can take a few business days, depending on the request's scope.
